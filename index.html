The Student Viability Framework

The FAD framework maps students across two independent axes — Academic Readiness and Developmental Readiness — to identify where they stand and what kind of college environment is most likely to help them thrive.

The student assessment begins with 20 fluid-reasoning items, then moves to 20 self-report questions on motivation, independence, and self-awareness. Results include an archetype profile and, optionally, a curated set of institution recommendations.

The Institutional Matrix

Colleges and universities differ not just in prestige or price but in the kind of learning environment they create. The institutional assessment measures Mission Intensity and Institutional Capability, placing an institution in one of four learning environment types.

Each type carries distinct implications for student outcomes, faculty culture, and strategic direction — and each comes with a recommended path toward stronger alignment between mission and capacity.

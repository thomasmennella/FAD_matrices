About This Assessment

This tool measures two independent dimensions — Academic Readiness (abstract reasoning, verbal ability, numerical thinking) and Developmental Readiness (executive function, emotional regulation, identity clarity, social fluency) — to place you on the Student Viability Matrix.

Your results reflect where you are right now — a developmental starting point, not a permanent verdict.
